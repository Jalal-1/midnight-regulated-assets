# Overview

What Midnight enables for regulated assets, and the building blocks every
product here composes.

- Programmable privacy: client-side proofs
- Shielded and unshielded assets, at both UTXO and contract level
- The OZ Compact modular library: access control, ECDSA multisig, confidential
  balances, note-based privacy, compliance hooks

The core claim, stated once here and then demonstrated twice in Products:
**every product is a different composition of these same blocks; the issuer
decides the composition.**

## The requirements checklist

Used unchanged on every product page:

- ECDSA 2-of-3 multisig custody
- Privacy, on a spectrum: value-private → fully graph-private
- Regulator visibility (viewing keys; disclose-to is platform roadmap)
- Controlled mint / burn
- Account segregation
- Recovery
- Compliance operations
